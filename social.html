The Community

Who this is for.

For Chicago-area creators, founders, athletes, artists, and independent professionals who are interested in other people’s work—and willing to contribute something of their own to the conversation. Follower count is not a gatekeeping criterion.

Formats we’re exploring

  • Small creator and founder conversations. Intimate, focused discussions around a specific challenge or project.
  • Informal meetups. Low-pressure gatherings organized around a shared local interest or hobby.
  • Media-adjacent gatherings. Optional, in-person extensions of a published Media conversation.
  • Participant-proposed collaborations. Spaces created by the community, for the community, with our logistical support.

* These are exploratory concepts, not a scheduled calendar of events.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is this a paid membership?

Not at this stage. This page simply collects interest as Texas Movement Social takes shape. Submitting the form is free.

Are there upcoming events?

No gatherings are currently announced here. You can express interest in future opportunities, and we will reach out when practical arrangements come together.

Do I need a large social following?

No. Interest in other people’s work and a willingness to participate meaningfully matter much more than audience size.

Will I be filmed or interviewed?

Expressing interest does not grant filming or interview permission. Any recording or publishing arrangements would be communicated and agreed upon separately.

Does participation include Media coverage?

No. Media coverage and Social participation are separate initiatives. One does not guarantee the other.

Can I propose a gathering?

Yes. Use the form to share your idea, intended audience, and what you could contribute. Please note that a proposal is not a confirmed partnership or guaranteed event.
