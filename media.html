The TMM Approach

One story, multiple entry points.

We design each story for different viewing experiences, ensuring depth for returning viewers and discovery for new ones.

Depth

The flagship conversation or reported feature. Develops context, personality, and substance for viewers who want the full picture.

Discovery

Short-form video highlighting one complete, compelling idea. Designed to give a new viewer an immediate reason to care.

Practical Value

Written summaries, carousels, or breakdowns that make a specific insight easy to revisit, save, or share.

Continuity

Follow-up features weeks or months later, showing what happened after the initial conversation and if the new processes held up.

Collaborate

Three distinct ways to work together.

We maintain strict separation between our editorial mission and our commercial services.

Editorial Publishing

TMM selects stories for its audience.

  • Researched conversation with a clear angle
  • Published feature or episode
  • 2–3 captioned clips for guest sharing
  • Approved promotional images/quote graphics
  • Clear attribution and coordinated publication

* We offer factual corrections, but retain final editorial control. Unpaid collaboration.

Creator Production

A client pays for specific content assets.

  • One focused recording session
  • One edited conversation or feature
  • A defined set of short-form assets
  • Client retains agreed-upon usage rights
  • Clear boundaries on advertising use

* Priced based on actual editing and coordination time. No unlimited content management.

Sponsored Programming

A sponsor funds clearly identified content.

  • Custom series or episode funding
  • Clearly labeled sponsorship integration
  • Agreed editorial boundaries
  • Transparent reporting on delivery

* We do not quietly sell "editorial features" as independent endorsements.
